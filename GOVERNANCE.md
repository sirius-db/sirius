# Sirius Governance

Sirius is a _GPU-native composable analytics engine_ under the Apache-2.0 license. Governance is intentionally minimal and will formalize as the project grows, with the goal of eventually moving under a neutral open source foundation. 

## Roles

- **Contributors**. Anyone can open issues or pull requests. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Maintainers** are trusted contributors who can review and merge contributions, make technical decisions, manage the maintainer base, and enforce the [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). They decide by consensus, falling back to a majority vote of maintainers when necessary. See [MAINTAINERS.md](./MAINTAINERS.md). 


## Becoming a Maintainer

1. A maintainer nominates a contributor with a short rationale and links to representative work (reviews and design input, not only authored PRs).
2. Lazy consensus over five working days — silence counts as approval. 
3. If a concern can't be resolved, a majority vote of maintainers decides.
4. On approval, a pull request adds the person to [MAINTAINERS.md](./MAINTAINERS.md) and access is granted.

Signals we look for: sustained contribution, sound judgment in review and design discussion, and reliability.

## Leaving

Maintainers may step down at any time by opening a pull request removing
themselves from [MAINTAINERS.md](./MAINTAINERS.md). 
Maintainers who haven't been active for a while may be moved to emeritus
in [MAINTAINERS.md](./MAINTAINERS.md) by other maintainers, and can return
by asking.
In rare cases, a maintainer may be removed by a majority vote of the other
maintainers.
