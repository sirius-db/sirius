Purpose: D-05 — Phase 22's chunked-pin design distributes parquet chunks across GPUs in round-robin order. Before this plan, the chunked_parquet_reader still routed reads through cudf's bundled file_source factory (kvikio backend), producing the same K.1-shaped race risk as site #1. After this plan, every parquet byte read by `pin_table` goes through the per-GPU uring ioctx.

Output: `src/sirius_extension.cpp` with the source_info construction at line 813 flipped from string-form to pointer-form, with proper lifetime management of `uring_io_object` and `sirius_datasource` across the reader's lifetime.

If chunked_parquet_reader rejects the pointer form at compile or runtime, escalate as a deviation in the plan SUMMARY — fall-back is non-chunked reader + cudf-side chunking, which would defeat Phase 22's chunked-pin design and require an architectural revisit.
